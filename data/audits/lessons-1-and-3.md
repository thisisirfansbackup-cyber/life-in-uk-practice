## Access limitations

Discovery search returned HTTP 402. Direct page checks were then attempted against known official domains. GOV.UK pages for jury service, elections, bank holidays, Crown Dependencies, terrorism reporting and NCS were readable; legislation.gov.uk’s Bill of Rights text was readable. Parliament and IWM pages returned 403; numerous guessed institutional page paths returned 404, and those are **not treated as evidence**. Accordingly, most historical/cultural items remain unconfirmed rather than being asserted from memory. The full paid guide was not accessed.
