## Bank-quality note (applies to many rows, not a factual error)

A large minority of L5/L6 items pad the option list with the correct answer plus a legal-sounding suffix — e.g. 101 "…Democracy Agreement / Treaty / Act", 110 "The Enlightenment Act / Treaty / Agreement", 113 "The Battle of Trafalgar Agreement / Act / Treaty", 119 "The Lord of the Rings Agreement / Act / Treaty", 138 "The Black Death Treaty / Agreement / Act", 140 "The Justice of the Peace Court Treaty / Act / Agreement". The answer is factually right, but three of four options are nonsense variants of the key, so the item tests nothing. Several other items are True/False stems whose distractors are "Partially false / Cannot be determined / All of the above" (100, 108, 116, 125, 132), or four-option stems where three options are "None of the above / All of the above / Cannot be determined" (98, 105, 112, 117, 118, 121, 124, 127, 144). Both patterns should be rebuilt as genuine four-way distractors; the evidence below supports the answer, not the option set.
