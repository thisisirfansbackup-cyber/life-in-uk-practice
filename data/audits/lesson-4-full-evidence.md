# Lesson 4: full official-source evidence audit (original questions 73–96)

**Checked:** 2026-11-21. **Primary source:** *Life in the UK: A guide for new residents*, 3rd edition, Crown copyright, 98-page PDF, `https://storage.googleapis.com/britizen-public/static/study-guide/document.pdf`. Section locators are recorded per runtime item. The full text was extracted and the relevant passages read. **Secondary official source:** Visit Wales national-symbols page (Crown copyright) for question 74; Ofcom Sections Five and Six for question 96.

## Findings

- Answers 73–95 are supported by the handbook, with wording tightened for clarity.
- **77:** handbook says Scottish and Northern Irish notes are valid everywhere in the UK, but shops need not accept them. The runtime question and explanation state that limitation.
- **82:** restored as a genuine select-two (Commons and Lords).
- **93:** the minimum age to stand as an MP is 18, not 21.
- **96:** the original “equal time to rival viewpoints” claim is wrong. Ofcom states due impartiality does not mean an equal division of time. Runtime asks which two broadcast media must follow due-impartiality rules, with television and radio as the two choices.
- **74:** Visit Wales identifies both the daffodil and leek as national symbols; the question asks for the flower, so daffodil is unambiguous.
- No unresolved factual blocker in this lesson.

## Limits

The handbook PDF is a third-party-hosted copy of the official text. Its edition, title and section headings identify the cited passages; no claim is made that the host is the UK government. Handbooks age: current-law questions elsewhere must be checked against current official guidance rather than assumed from this edition.
