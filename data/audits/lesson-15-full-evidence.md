## Findings

- Answers 336–359 are supported, with corrupted options replaced and four distinct choices.
- **336, 344, 349, 350 and 353** are genuine select-two questions and require exactly two choices.
- **344 (targeted follow-up, 2026-11-23):** restricted to England/Wales and two alternative court compositions, not “TWO people”: three magistrates or a district judge. [GOV.UK — Youth courts](https://www.gov.uk/courts/youth-courts), composition. This does not describe Northern Ireland.
- **356 (targeted duplicate correction, 2026-11-23):** asks when the Slavery Abolition Act was passed (1833), not when slavery ended throughout the Empire. [National Archives — The 1833 Abolition of Slavery Act](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/explore-the-collection/explore-by-time-period/georgians/1833-abolition-of-slavery-act-and-compensation-claims) supports passage and transition; [enacted s.64](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/1833/act/73/section/64/enacted/en/html) excludes East India Company territories, Ceylon and Saint Helena. Prepared English and Roman Urdu now qualify the scope.
- **338:** England was a republic from 1649 to 1660, after Charles I was executed.
- **341:** the First World War ended at 11:00 am on 11 November 1918.
- **354:** the minimum age for a motorcycle is 17; a moped can be ridden from 16.
- **359:** domestic violence is a serious crime whether the parties are married or cohabiting.
- No unresolved factual blocker in this lesson.
