## Limits and time sensitivity

The paid official Life in the UK guide was not publicly accessible and was **not read**. Exa/web search returned HTTP 402/429 during this run; Parliament.uk and Royal.uk pages attempted via direct fetch returned 403, and a GOV.UK national-parks URL returned 404. Accordingly, most historical/cultural items are explicitly left unconfirmed rather than inferred from memory. The five-year election interval is current GOV.UK wording (“usually”), while legal assertions are tied to the accessed statute/GOV.UK pages as of 2026-11-07; the guide may preserve older phrasing or facts.
