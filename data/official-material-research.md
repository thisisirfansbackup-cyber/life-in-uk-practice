# Official Life in the UK test material — research note

**Access date:** 2026-11-07

## Findings

- **Current official core study book:** *Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents*, **3rd edition**. The official Home Office/TSO-branded shop product is titled “Official Guide for New Residents book”; its product page identifies the 3rd edition, ISBN **9780113413409**, paperback, and publishing date **28 January 2013**. The official shop identifies TSO (The Stationery Office) through its Home Office/TSO branding and TSO copyright. It links book, eBook and audio formats.
- **Official URL(s):** GOV.UK preparation guidance: <https://www.gov.uk/life-in-the-uk-test/study-for-the-test> (the page currently resolves as the Life in the UK Test booking guide, with “Prepare for the test” section); official shop: <https://www.officiallifeintheuk.co.uk/shop>; core guide product: <https://www.officiallifeintheuk.co.uk/official-guide-new-residents-book>.
- **Question-bank qualification:** GOV.UK says candidates are tested only on information in the official Guide for New Residents and advises studying it. The official publisher’s core guide page explicitly says the guide contains no mock questions. Separately, TSO sells an “Official Practice Questions & Answers” supplement with **400+ practice questions** based on the guide. Neither GOV.UK nor the official product pages inspected state that this is the complete/live exam question bank, or that all test questions are published. Therefore describe official provision as study material plus a substantial official practice-question supplement—not as a verified complete exam-bank release. The pages do not explicitly state “there is no complete bank,” so that stronger negative claim is unverified.
- **Official test format:** **24 questions, 45 minutes**. GOV.UK’s “What happens at the test” page says exactly that and says questions are based on the handbook. The GOV.UK booking/preparation guide independently repeats 24 questions and 45 minutes.

## Supporting evidence

1. **[Tier A — official government]** GOV.UK says only information from the official Guide for New Residents is tested, and lists book/eBook/audio and official e-learning/practice app options. **Measurement:** direct page text (no numerical measurement). Source: “Life in the UK Test — Book the Life in the UK Test” (<https://www.gov.uk/life-in-the-uk-test/study-for-the-test>, accessed 2026-11-07).
2. **[Tier A — official publisher]** Official product page identifies the core guide as 3rd edition, ISBN 9780113413409, dated 28 Jan 2013, and says it contains essential knowledge but no mock questions. **Measurement:** product-page metadata and description. Source: “Official Guide for New Residents book” (<https://www.officiallifeintheuk.co.uk/official-guide-new-residents-book>, accessed 2026-11-07).
3. **[Tier A — official publisher]** The official practice book is described as a supplement, with 400+ questions based on the official guide; it says it contains practice questions only and no study material. **Measurement:** publisher’s stated count, not an independently counted question set. Source: “Official Practice Questions & Answers Book” (<https://www.officiallifeintheuk.co.uk/official-practice-questions-answers-book>, accessed 2026-11-07).
4. **[Tier A — official government]** The test is 24 questions in 45 minutes, based on the Life in the UK handbook. **Measurement:** stated official test conditions. Source: “What happens at the test” (<https://www.gov.uk/life-in-the-uk-test/what-happens-test>, accessed 2026-11-07).
5. **[Tier A — official publisher]** Official shop landing page distinguishes the guide from the practice Q&A product and identifies the shop as TSO (Home Office/TSO logos; TSO copyright). **Measurement:** current live catalogue and page branding. Source: “Official Life in the UK Learning — The Official Shop” (<https://www.officiallifeintheuk.co.uk/shop>, accessed 2026-11-07).

## Conflicts or uncertainty

- The official book’s original publishing date is 2013, but the publisher labels the currently sold product “3rd edition” and provides a “latest impression” amendments PDF linked on its product page. This research verifies the current edition/product metadata, not the date of the latest physical print impression or a full audit of the amendment PDF.
- “400+” is the publisher’s description of its practice book; no claim that these are every question used in the live test was found on the official pages inspected.

## Unverified claims

- Whether a complete question bank exists internally or is available through another official channel: not established. No inspected official source describes a complete/live test question bank.
- Whether all content in every current impression has been amended identically: not independently checked.

## Blocked sources

- No required official page was inaccessible; GOV.UK and the official publisher pages loaded directly. Web search returned HTTP 402, so discovery search could not be used; this was bypassed by fetching known official GOV.UK and publisher URLs directly. No non-official sources were used.
