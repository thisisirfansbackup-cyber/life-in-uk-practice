# Official Life in the UK guide: latest-impression amendments

**Access date:** 2026-11-07. **Scope:** official publisher’s linked amendments PDF plus official primary government/statistical sources for time-sensitive examples.

## Findings

The live TSO/Home Office-branded product page for the *Official Guide for New Residents* (3rd edition; first published 28 January 2013) links a one-page PDF, “Life in the UK – A Guide for New Residents: Latest impression amendments.” Its filename ends `03012025.pdf`; PDF metadata says created/modified 3 January 2025. It compiles corrections for impressions 23, 26 and 27.

**Changes listed in the PDF (by impression):**

- **23:** replace the Queen with King Charles III in the introductory image references, Remembrance Day/Cenotaph reference, pages 121–123 and Commonwealth section; insert a paragraph on Queen Elizabeth II’s death.
- **26:** update titles/names for Ian Botham, Andrew Lloyd-Webber, Tim Rice and Edwin Lutyens; remove “(Man)” from Booker Prize reference; remove “Consort”; update Senedd membership to **60 members (SMs)**; update polling-station information; update small-claims maximums; revise the glossary definition of “rural.”
- **27:** update David Weir’s London Marathon wins, add England women’s football team winning the Euros, update Booker Prize name, and revise information on contacting elected members, joining the electoral register, Council of Europe membership, Northern Ireland small-claims maximum and applying online for an NI number.

**Currency assessment: not sufficient to establish that the 2013 book is up to date as of 2026-11-07.** It is a finite errata list, not a dated full revision or a comprehensive current-facts/legal audit. In particular, its stated **60 Senedd members** is superseded by the 2024 Senedd Cymru (Members and Elections) Act’s structure of **16 constituencies × 6 members = 96**. The PDF contains no update to population or workforce statistics. As examples of how fast such figures move, the ONS’s latest mid-year estimate (released 1 October 2026) puts UK population at **69,483,900 at mid-2025**; its October 2026 labour-market bulletin (released 20 October) estimates an employment rate of **75.3% for ages 16–64 in June–August 2026**. These are current official statistics, not assertions about what the guide or lessons state.

The PDF does list some targeted legal/administrative changes (polling, small-claims thresholds, electoral registration, NI-number applications), but it does not claim to review all laws. For example, the Marriage and Civil Partnership (Minimum Age) Act 2022 changed the minimum marriage/civil-partnership age in England and Wales to 18; that Act is not mentioned in the amendments PDF. This demonstrates that the list is not a complete legal update, but I cannot say whether this fact appears in the supplied lessons.

## Important coverage limitation

The project checkout contains no supplied 17-lesson text; `data/questions.json` is empty. Consequently I could not map the amendment list or current official facts to particular lesson claims, nor determine which lesson-specific population/workforce, Parliament/government, election-cycle or legal claims need correction. The Senedd discrepancy above is between the publisher’s PDF and the later enacted statutory structure—not a verified discrepancy in the absent lesson text. No claim about lesson content should be inferred from this note.
