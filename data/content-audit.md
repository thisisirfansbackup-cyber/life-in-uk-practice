# Official material and content audit

## Answer authority

- **Core guide:** *Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents*, 3rd edition, ISBN 9780113413409. The current official publisher product page lists this edition and links a latest-impression amendments PDF. Publisher: The Stationery Office (TSO), on the official Home Office/TSO-branded Life in the UK shop.
- **Government guidance:** GOV.UK says test questions are based on the official Guide for New Residents and advises candidates to study it.
- **Official practice supplement:** TSO's *Official Practice Questions & Answers Book* is described as containing 400+ questions based on the guide. The publisher does not describe it as a complete/live exam question bank.
- **Access date:** 2026-11-07.
- **Currentness:** The publisher's 3rd-edition page links a finite latest-impression amendments PDF (impressions 23, 26, 27). It is not a comprehensive 2026 legal/factual update. Use the guide for stable study content, but when current law or an authoritative government update conflicts with it, use the later current primary source and record the conflict here.
- **Research notes:** `data/official-material-research.md` contains source URLs and citations for the edition, official practice supplement, and exam format. `data/current-amendments-research.md` describes the amendments PDF and its currency limitations.

## Verification method

The complete guide is not publicly available from the official sources checked. As agreed with the user, use accessible official government, legal, statistical, and public-institution sources to verify claims. Each bank entry records a specific source locator and check date. If no accessible official source supports a lesson's claim, record it as unverified and do not treat the lesson note alone as verification. The learner-facing question will use corrected content without a correction warning.

## Current bank coverage

The current bank contains **all 407 source positions** from the supplied 17 lessons, in original order. The full release gate (`npm run check:bank:full`) refuses a partial, untranslated, unconfirmed, or source-number-mismatched bank. The earlier 51-question published bank is archived at `data/audits/legacy-published-bank.json` for migration review.

## Handbook source limitation

Many lesson 4–17 items cite *Life in the UK: A guide for new residents*, 3rd edition (Crown copyright), using an accessible PDF copy hosted at `https://storage.googleapis.com/britizen-public/static/study-guide/document.pdf`. That URL is a **third-party mirror of the official text**, not a government publication page. The edition, title and section locators identify the cited passages, and the full text was read during verification. Items whose only source is this handbook are therefore recorded as `corrected` rather than `verified`; the distinction is intentional. Current-law or changed-institution items use current official government, Parliament, regulator, Senedd or legislation pages instead. The paid official guide was not purchased or accessed through a government portal.
