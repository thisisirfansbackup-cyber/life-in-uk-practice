# Official material and content audit

## Answer authority

- **Core guide:** *Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents*, 3rd edition, ISBN 9780113413409. The current official publisher product page lists this edition and links a latest-impression amendments PDF. Publisher: The Stationery Office (TSO), on the official Home Office/TSO-branded Life in the UK shop.
- **Government guidance:** GOV.UK says test questions are based on the official Guide for New Residents and advises candidates to study it.
- **Official practice supplement:** TSO's *Official Practice Questions & Answers Book* is described as containing 400+ questions based on the guide. The publisher does not describe it as a complete/live exam question bank.
- **Access date:** 2026-11-07.
- **Research note:** `data/official-material-research.md` contains source URLs and citations for the edition, official practice supplement, and exam format. A separate check of the latest-impression amendments is in progress.

## Verification method

Every included answer must be supported by the official guide or an official update to it. Each bank entry records a specific chapter/section or amendment locator and check date. If an official source does not support a lesson's claim, do not silently treat the lesson as proof: resolve the discrepancy against the official material or exclude the item until it can be verified. The learner-facing question will use the corrected content without a correction warning.
