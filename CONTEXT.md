# Life in the UK Practice — Domain Context

## Practice flow

- **Question check**: The learner submits a selected answer for one question. A checked question has immediate right/wrong feedback and its response is locked for the rest of that test.
- **Select-two question**: A question requiring exactly two choices. The learner must select two choices before checking it; correctness compares the selected pair with the correct pair.
- **Test completion**: The learner finishes a study block or revision round after checking each question in it. Completion produces the overall result and updates long-term revision history.
- **Unfinished test**: A practice session the learner has not completed. Its question order, position, selected answers, and checked status remain resumable in that browser; it does not yet update long-term revision history.

## Study coverage

- **Question collection**: All 407 numbered questions from the supplied 17 lessons, kept in their original order even when two questions cover similar facts.
- **Study block**: A fixed, ordered part of the question collection: seven blocks of 51 questions followed by one block of 50. Revision questions do not replace its questions.
- **Revision round**: Separate practice that brings back previously missed questions, prioritising questions the learner repeatedly gets wrong; it does not advance study-block completion.
